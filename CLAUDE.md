## 4. FOOD PLANNING

### Weekly meal plan structure:

Every week we plan:

- **Breakfast:** No cooked breakfast — protein shake with collagen and matcha, every day. No recipe planning needed for this.
- **2 lunch options** — batch prepped, 4 portions each (2 for Niki, 2 for husband). 30g+ protein. Max 5–10 mins to assemble.
  - **Lunch 1 — Salad:** Cold, batch-prepped grain or pasta salad. Things like quinoa, orzo, farro or giant couscous with a protein source (fish, edamame, halloumi, legumes). Dressable the night before.
  - **Lunch 2 — Pitta/wrap:** A batch-prepped protein filling (e.g. shredded chicken, tuna smash, spiced turkey) that works with pitta, wrap or flatbread. Keeps 3–4 days in the fridge.
  - At least **one of the two lunches must be vegetarian** each week.
- **3 dinner options** — cook once, eat twice (double batch = 4 adult portions + son's half portions). Each dinner made one night, reheated the next. 30g+ protein. Fixed weekly format:
  - **1 pasta dish**
  - **1 rice or other grain dish**
  - **1 traybake** — protein flexible (chicken, sausages/pork, fish, etc. — not fixed to chicken)
  - At least **one of the three dinners must be vegetarian** each week.
- **1 baking option** — refined sugar free, suitable for a toddler, **no chocolate**

### Cook once eat twice structure:

|Night    |What's happening            |
|---------|----------------------------|
|Monday   |Cook Dinner 1 (double batch)|
|Tuesday  |Reheat Dinner 1 leftovers   |
|Wednesday|Cook Dinner 2 (double batch)|
|Thursday |Reheat Dinner 2 leftovers   |
|Friday   |Cook Dinner 3 (double batch)|
|Saturday |Reheat Dinner 3 leftovers   |
|Sunday   |Free / flexible             |

### Portion sizes:

- Niki + husband = 2 adult portions each meal
- Son = half an adult portion
- Total per recipe = 4.5 adult portion equivalents (round up for shopping)

### Dietary rules:

- **No beef**
- Mix preferred: 1–2 meat, 1–2 fish, rest vegetarian
- Niki does **not** drink cow's milk — needs easy dairy substitutions in recipes
- Husband and son **do** have cow's milk
- Niki does **not** like boiled, scrambled, poached or fried eggs — eggs in recipes (baking, frittata etc.) are fine
- Toddler eats **dinners only** — adapt recipes for him where needed
- All meals must be **easy and quick to prepare**
- Prefer **tray bakes and one-pan dishes** to minimise washing up
- No repeat or similar meals in the same week — keep proteins, flavours and formats varied
- Husband does **not** eat fish — always provide a meat swap for fish dishes (usually chicken)
- Aim for **30g+ protein per meal**

### Recipe sources:

Emily English (emthenutritionist), Ottolenghi, Instagram recipes. Always search for real recipes with links — don't generate generic recipes.

### Store cupboard check:

Always check in on store cupboard staples before assuming we have them. Ask what's in the fridge each week to use things up.

### Weekly staples (always on the Ocado order):

- Full fat milk, kefir, soya yoghurt, oat milk
- Kimchi, cheddar cheese, sheep's cheese, weetabix
